About the UBWC

01 / OVERVIEW

The UltraBullet World Championship is being developed as an international team competition with qualification stages and a final championship event.

The project has been moved to the Christmas and New Year break to allow sufficient preparation. The proposed schedule keeps the competition compact; exact dates and registration details will be confirmed by the organizers.

Competition format

02 / FORMAT
01

Qualifier

One open qualifying stage. The best 20 teams advance to the next round.

26 DEC 2026 · PROPOSED
02

Quarterfinal & semifinal

The field is reduced from 20 to 15 teams, then from 15 to the 10 finalists.

27–28 DEC 2026 · PROPOSED
03

Championship final

Ten teams compete across three final days, with a short New Year break.

29–30 DEC 2026 & 02 JAN 2027
04

Individual champion

The individual title is determined from the official final Lichess score of each eligible player.
