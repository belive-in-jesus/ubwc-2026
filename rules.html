OFFICIAL FORMAT

Rules & format.

The core rules currently defined for the UBWC.

01

Tournament format

Every UBWC tournament consists of three separate 90-minute UltraBullet Battles, with a 5–10 minute break between Battles.

02

Teams

Each team may use a maximum of 15 players. Players may change teams between tournaments or between Battles, subject to Lichess restrictions.

03

Qualification

The First Qualifier is open to all teams. Exactly 20 teams advance. The Quarterfinals reduce 20 to 15; the Semifinals reduce 15 to 10.

04

Team ranking

Placement is the primary ranking criterion. If placement is tied, the official Lichess score is the secondary criterion. Remaining ties are resolved using Lichess's impartial random tie-break system.

05

Finals

The ten finalists compete across three days. Each day contains three separate 90-minute Battles, for nine Final Battles in total.

06

Individual champion

The Individual UBWC World Champion is determined from the official final Lichess score of the player's account. Team placement is irrelevant.

07

Account policy

Multi-accounting is not permitted. Only one Lichess account per player may be considered. Using additional accounts provides no advantage for the Individual Championship and is prohibited for both team and individual competition.

08

Fair play & technical issues

Standard Lichess rules and systems apply to disconnects, technical issues, cheating, fair-play violations and account restrictions.
